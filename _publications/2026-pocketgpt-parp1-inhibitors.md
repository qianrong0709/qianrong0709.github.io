PocketGPT is a pocket-conditioned generative framework that jointly incorporates protein-pocket context, physicochemical property targets, fragment priors, and protein–ligand interaction fingerprints. In a prospective design–make–test campaign, the framework identified a novel PARP1 inhibitor with nanomolar binding affinity and favorable tolerability in mice.
